// Why CAPRUX

From CAPRUK,
to CAPRUX.

In Sundanese, capruk means scattered. Unhinged. Going nowhere fast. It's the phase when everything falls apart and nothing makes sense. Sounds bad? It is. But honestly — every single one of us has been there.

Why the "X"? Not a typo. Not aesthetic. The letter X is a plot twist — two lines crossing, proving that chaos can flip into power. X also reads as "eks" (ex). CAPRUX = Former Capruk. Once broken, now building.

Born in a cramped room in Baleendah, with a head full of noise and hands that couldn't stay still, CAPRUX grows from the roots of Sundanese philosophy. But the feeling of being "capruk" — that's universal. From Jawa to Batak, from Bugis to Papua. Different islands, same story: you fell apart, then chose to rise. That's why our designs always carry etnik Nusantara pride.

CAPRUX wasn't born from a clean plan. It's the result of fighting overthinking, laziness, and ego — day after day. Doing the Impossible, with GOD's Help. We go hard, and trust the sky to handle the rest.
